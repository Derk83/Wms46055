Equipment request guide

How to request equipment and track it through review and fulfillment.

Start an equipment request

1. Enter request details

Enter when the equipment is needed, the destination, purpose, project, and priority. If there is an end date, it must be on or after the start date.

Choose whether substitutes are acceptable and add requester notes for delivery, access, or operating requirements.

2. Choose equipment

Add one line for each type of equipment. Choose a category, then optionally select a Preferred item when an available asset is shown.

If the equipment is not listed, describe it in Unlisted equipment. A preferred asset is optional—the equipment team can allocate a suitable item during review.

3. Submit your request

Review quantities, dates, and destination, then select Submit request. Keep the request number from the confirmation page for questions or follow-up.

4. Track your request

Open My requests to see every request and its current status. Each request explains what happens next. The normal approval path is Submitted, Under review, Approved, Ready, and Fulfilled; a request may instead be Declined or Cancelled.

Open My requests

5. Edit or cancel

You can edit a request while its status is Submitted. You can cancel it while it is Submitted or Under review. After review progresses, contact the equipment team and include the request number if something changes.

Need warehouse material instead?

Consumable and warehouse-stock material is requested in the separate Material Requests app.

Open the material request guide — a separate sign-in may be required.

Need assistance?

Contact your equipment manager through your normal workplace support channel and include the request number.
