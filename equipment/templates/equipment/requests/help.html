How equipment requests are reviewed and fulfilled.

Submitting a request

Describe the equipment, quantity, destination, and dates. You do not need to choose a specific asset.

After submission

You can edit a submitted request. Once review begins, contact the equipment team if details change. Status guidance appears on each request.

Need assistance?

Contact your equipment manager through your normal workplace support channel and include the request number.
