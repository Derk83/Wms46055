Weekly report (auto-delivered)

The weekly activity report is automatically generated and emailed to managers every Friday at 7:00 AM America/Chicago. It covers the previous Monday through Sunday.

Generated PDFs are archived at media/auto_reports/weekly/ on the WMS host.

About these reports

Reports are generated live from the production database. The daily page supports:

  • Quick presets: Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, This month, Last month
  • Custom date picker for ad-hoc reviews
  • PDF download for printing or sharing

Only superusers and members of the Logistics Manager, Sr. Logistics Manager, and Procurement Manager groups can view these reports. Logistics Specialists, Procurement Specialists, Requesters, and Viewers cannot.
